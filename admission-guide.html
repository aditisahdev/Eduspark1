Admission Guide

Admission information, step by step

From choosing a course to confirming your seat.

  1. Choose a course

    Start with a category, then narrow to a course and specialization. Explore courses →

  2. Shortlist colleges

    Filter by state, city and course, and compare the ones that fit. Browse colleges →

  3. Check the details with the institution

    Confirm the programme, approvals, fees and whether admissions are open. Our listings are a starting point, not a confirmation.

  4. Prepare your documents

    Keep your exam score or rank, class 12 marks and ID ready. Each institution lists its own full document list.

  5. Apply and track deadlines

    Note the application, counselling and fee dates for every college you apply to.

  6. Confirm your seat

    Pay the fee and collect the receipt only through the institution's official process.

Keep these ready

  • Exam score or rank
  • Class 12 marks
  • Your budget
  • Cities you prefer
