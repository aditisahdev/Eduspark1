Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

This policy explains what personal information Eduspark Admissions collects through this website, why we collect it and the choices you have. By using the site or sending us an enquiry, you agree to this policy.

What we collect

  • Your name and phone number, when you fill in an enquiry form
  • The course you are interested in and, if you choose to share it, your exam score or rank and a short message
  • Your email address, when you subscribe to updates

We do not ask for payment details, passwords or identity documents on this website.

How your details reach us

This website does not store form entries in a database. When you submit the enquiry form or the subscribe box, your device opens WhatsApp with a message already filled in. The message reaches us only when you send it in WhatsApp. WhatsApp handles that message under its own terms and privacy policy.

How we use your details

  • To reply to your enquiry and give you counselling
  • To help you shortlist colleges, check forms and track deadlines
  • To send admission alerts and updates, if you subscribed

We do not sell your personal information.

Sharing

We share your details with a college or university only when you ask us to apply or enquire on your behalf. We may also share information when the law requires it.

Third-party services

  • WhatsApp, used for enquiries and subscriptions as described above
  • Google Fonts, which loads the typefaces on this site. Your browser requests them from Google, which can see your IP address
  • Links to other websites, such as official institution sites. We are not responsible for their privacy practices

Cookies and tracking

This website does not currently use advertising cookies or analytics tools. If that changes, we will update this policy.

Your choices

You can ask us to stop messaging you, to correct your details or to delete the details we hold about you. Contact us using the details on the Contact page, and we will act on your request.

Students under 18

If you are under 18, please involve a parent or guardian when you share your details with us.

Security

We take reasonable care to protect the information you share with us. No method of sending information over the internet is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it last changed.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Reach us through the Contact page.
